Water Drop

Forty-one drops of clear epoxy resting on a thin acrylic sheet. Each outline is first printed as a fine white line, about 0.1 mm wide; the epoxy is then dropped inside it and stops exactly at the line.

Held only by surface tension, every drop settles into its own rounded dome, about 1.2 to 1.6 mm high, and bends the light that passes through it.

Made by Alltäglich, swimming goods that bring the feeling of the pool into everyday life.
