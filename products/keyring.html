수영장 레인 키링

Pool Lane Keyring

A keyring cast in clear resin over a printed pool floor, about 30 × 46 × 7.8 mm. The rippled top bends the tiles and the lane line beneath, as if you were looking down into the water.

The back is finished with a clear dome. A stainless eye pin is set into the top edge and carries a chain of jump rings, a figure-eight swivel, an engraved tag and a snap hook.

Made by Alltäglich, swimming goods that bring the feeling of the pool into everyday life.
