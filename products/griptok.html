Pool Grip Tok

A MagSafe grip with a clear resin dome cast over a printed pool floor. The rippled surface bends the light, so the tiles and the lane mark beneath seem to sway as if seen through water.

The dome is 43 mm across and rises to about 14 mm at its crest. A clear two-stage accordion folds flat against the phone and opens to hold it.

Made by Alltäglich, swimming goods that bring the feeling of the pool into everyday life.
